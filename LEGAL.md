## What we ship

We ship our own code. You supply a disc you own. The behavior reference is the PlayStation USA release, serial SLUS-00491. You read that executable in Ghidra on your machine.

We do not copy decompiled source into the tree. Raw decompiled C stays under `build/` on your machine. Quoting one address in a note is fine.

## United States

In the United States, courts have treated reverse engineering of a program you own, to learn unprotected ideas and write a new program, as fair use. The case people cite is Sega Enterprises Ltd. v. Accolade, Inc., 977 F.2d 1510 (9th Cir. 1992). Copyright in the original program remains. This repo is our code, plus notes that cite addresses.

## Other countries

Other countries differ. Some allow this kind of study. Some limit it. Read the law where you live before you trace an executable or publish notes.
