# Caller clear path at 0x800650f4

The [released path](call-800650e0.md) tests bit `0x40` of complemented
input `a0` at `0x800650e4`. `beq` takes `0x800650f4` when that result is
zero. The delay slot at `0x800650e8` sets `v0` to `0x7f` on either side.
This note starts at the taken block. It does not resume the parked static
boot.

The [Up path](call-800650d0.md) stores `0x80` at `s0+0xb` in the delay
slot of `j` to `0x800650f8`. This path stores zero at that byte before
joining the same tail.

`python3 tools/boot_slice.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x800650f4 --max 128`
reports 18 instructions and stops at `bne` `0x8006518c`. The report stays
in `build/`.

At `0x800650f4`, `sb` stores zero at `s0+0xb`. `sb` at `0x800650f8` stores
zero at `s0+0x13`. `sb` at `0x800650fc` stores zero at `s0+0x14`. `lw` at
`0x80065100` loads `a1` from `sp+0x58`. That word is not stated. The
delay-slot value `0x7f` is not stored in this span.

`jal` at `0x80065104` targets [call-8006471c](call-8006471c.md). The delay
slot copies `s5` into `a0`. `ra` is `0x8006510c`. The named helper body is
not entered here.

After the call, `j` at `0x8006510c` targets `0x8006516c`. The delay slot
at `0x80065110` adds `0x7c` to `s0`. The tail at `0x8006516c` adds four to
`s4`, one to `s5`, shifts `fp` left, and adds one to `s7`. It loads words
from `sp+0x28` and `sp+0x1c`. Their live values are not stated. It adds
four to the `sp+0x28` word and stores it back in the delay slot of the
next branch.

`bne` at `0x8006518c` compares `s7` with the limit from `sp+0x1c`. The
taken target is `0x80064e40`. Fallthrough is `0x80065194`. The limit word
and taken side are unstated. This note stops at that branch. It does not
enter either path.

## Our code

`components/exe/call_800650f4.cpp` clears bytes at offsets `0x0b`,
`0x13`, and `0x14`, calls `call_8006471c`, advances the record pointer by
`0x7c`, and updates the scan counters through the delay slot before the
branch recorded in [call-8006518c.md](call-8006518c.md).

## Gap

The fallthrough at `0x800650ec` is recorded in
[call-800650ec.md](call-800650ec.md). The loop branch at
`0x8006518c` is recorded in [call-8006518c.md](call-8006518c.md). It loops
to `0x80064e40` when `s7` is signed-less-than the word at `sp+0x1c`. That
word stays unstated. Words at `sp+0x58` and `sp+0x28` stay unstated.
Caller resume at `0x80065194` and the body at `0x80064e40` are not covered
here.

The taken target is recorded in [call-80064e40.md](call-80064e40.md). That
block clears `s6`, builds the `PadGetState` port from `sp+0x18` and `s7`,
and stops at the unknown `beq` at `0x80064e60`. It does not enter either
side of this `bne`.
