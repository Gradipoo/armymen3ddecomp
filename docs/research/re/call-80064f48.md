# Caller block at 0x80064f48

The [byte compare](call-80064f14.md) takes this path when the second
masked byte is `0x41`. This note covers the measured block through the
mask stores at `0x80064f8c`. It stops at the `bne` at `0x80064fa8`. It
does not resume the parked static boot.

At `0x80064f48`, the caller ORs the word at `sp+0x20` with `fp`, then
loads an unsigned halfword through `s1` into `a1` and advances `s1` by
two. It loads the word at `0x800dd730` and compares it with four at
`0x80064f70`. The delay slot forms the bitwise complement of `a1` in
`a0`.

Captured menu samples had that word zero compared with four at
`0x80064f70`, so the branch took `0x80064f8c`. The
halfword was `0xffff` in most samples or `0xffef` during an observed Up
action. This note does not assign other bit meanings.

At `0x80064f8c`, the caller reads the old halfword at `s0+4`, stores
the complemented low half of `a0` there, and stores derived halfwords at
offsets six and eight from `s0`, matching the table in
[call-80064f24](call-80064f24.md). The `bne` at `0x80064fa8` compares an
unchanged `0x41` in `a2` with `0x73`. The taken target is `0x8006508c`.
This slice does not follow either side.

## Our code

`components/exe/call_80064f48.cpp` ORs the stack word, reads a halfword
from a host table index, requires compare word not equal to four for the measured
path, applies the three halfword stores on the record, and returns the
complemented low bits for later bit tests. Integration in
`menu_input_scan_step` follows the taken `bne` at `0x80064fa8` to
[call-8006508c.md](call-8006508c.md) when the masked second byte is `0x41`.

## Gap

The compare word at `0x800dd730` when nonzero is not followed.
