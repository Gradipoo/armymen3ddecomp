# Block at 0x8007a4e4

Traced fact from the USA PS-X EXE. Re-check on a disc you own with `python3 tools/entry_disasm.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x8007a4e4`. The executable and the listing stay in `build/`.

The start address is `0x8007a4e4`. The `jal` in [call-8007a540-2.md](call-8007a540-2.md) jumps here. `a0` is `0x8007c4a4`. `ra` is `0x8007a5a4`. The frame at `0x8007a540` stays open. The frame at `0x8007a628` stays open. The frame at `0x800251f8` stays open. Its saved `ra` stays `0x8007faec`.

`lui` at `0x8007a4e4` and `addiu` at `0x8007a4e8` build `0x8007f9a8`. `lw` at `0x8007a4ec` loads the word at `0x8007f9b0` into `a1`. That word is `0x80010000`. `lw` at `0x8007a4f0` loads the word at `0x8007f9b4` into `a3`. That word is `0x8001007c`. `addu` at `0x8007a500` sets `a2` to 0. `a1` is below `a3`, so `beq` at `0x8007a4fc` is not taken.

The loop at `0x8007a504` loads the word at `a1`, then the word at that pointer. `bne` at `0x8007a514` continues when that word is not `a0`. The delay slot is `nop`. On a match, `addu` at `0x8007a51c` copies the pointer into `a2`. `addiu` at `0x8007a520` adds 4 to `a1`. While `a1` stays below `a3` and `a2` stays 0, `beq` at `0x8007a530` returns to `0x8007a504`.

The match is the pointer `0x800b5d78` from the slot at `0x80010058`. The word at `0x800b5d78` is `0x8007c4a4`. After that copy, `a1` is `0x8001005c`. `a2` is not 0, so `beq` at `0x8007a530` is not taken. `jr` at `0x8007a538` returns through `ra`. The delay slot copies `a2` into `v0`. `v0` leaves as `0x800b5d78`. `ra` leaves as `0x8007a5a4`.

The same bytes run again for a later `a0`. This note states the first call only. Control goes to `0x8007a5a4`. The caller return at `0x8007faec` is not traced.
