# Block at 0x8008789c

Traced fact from the USA PS-X EXE. Re-check on a disc you own with `python3 tools/entry_disasm.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x8008789c`. The executable and the listing stay in `build/`.

The start address is `0x8008789c`. It is not in `tools/sdk_names.txt`. `python3 tools/sdk_match.py` does not name it. The `jal` in [call-8008760c.md](call-8008760c.md) calls here and sets `ra` to `0x80087634`. On arrival `a0` is 0 and `a1` is `0xf0`. `s0` is `0x800e15f0`. `s1` is `0x800e160c`. The tool stopped at the first `bltz` because it did not have `a0`. This note uses that `a0`. Both `bltz` tests are not taken. The hard cap is 64 instructions. This block ends at `jr ra` before the cap.

`sll` and `sra` by 16 sign-extend `a0`. `a0` is 0, so `a2` is 0 and `bltz` at `0x800878a4` is not taken. The delay slot sets `v0` to 0. `lh` at `0x800878b0` loads the halfword at `0x800b75cc`. That address is inside the text image. The halfword is 0. No note stores it. `addiu` makes `v0` `-1`. `slt` of `-1` against `a2` (0) leaves `v0` at 1. `lhu` reloads the same halfword, so `a2` is 0. `bne` at `0x800878c8` is taken. The delay slot sets `v0` to `-1`. `addu` at `0x800878d4` copies that into `a0`, so `a0` is `-1`.

`sll` and `sra` by 16 sign-extend `a1`. `a1` is `0xf0`, so `a2` is `0xf0` and `bltz` at `0x800878e0` is not taken. `lh` at `0x800878ec` loads the halfword at `0x800b75ce`. That halfword is 0. `addiu` makes `v0` `-1`. `slt` against `0xf0` leaves `v0` at 1. `lhu` reloads 0 into `a2`. `beq` at `0x80087904` is not taken. The delay slot keeps the low 10 bits of `a1`, so `v1` is `0xf0`. `j` at `0x8008790c` goes to `0x80087918`. The delay slot sets `a1` to `-1`. `andi` at `0x80087918` keeps the low 10 bits of that `-1`, so `v1` is `0x3ff`. `sll` by 10 leaves `v1` as `0xffc00`. `andi` keeps the low 10 bits of `a0` (`-1`), so `v0` is `0x3ff`. `lui` writes `0xe3000000` into `a0`. `or` joins that into `v0`.

`jr` at `0x80087928` returns through `ra`. The delay slot joins `v1` into `v0`, so `v0` is `0xe30fffff`. `ra` is `0x80087634`. This block does not store that word.

After the delay slot, control goes to `0x80087634`. `v0` is `0xe30fffff`. The words there are not traced. The display x and y halfwords are still not quoted. This note does not close the gap in `components/exe/load.cpp`.
