# Call at 0x8007a250

Traced fact from the USA PS-X EXE. Re-check on a disc you own with
`python3 tools/entry_disasm.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x8007a250`.
The executable and the listing stay in `build/`.

This is the callee of the `jal` in [call-8007a384.md](call-8007a384.md).
Arriving `a0` is `0x8009f368`, `a1` is `1`, `a2` is `0x80010db0`, and `a3`
is `0`. Arriving `ra` is `0x8007a3b0`.

The start address is `0x8007a250`. This is the function entry. The tool
follows straight-line words until the first branch. It followed 2
instructions and stopped at the `beq`. The hard cap is 64 instructions.
There is no `jal` or `jalr`. This note continues through `jr ra`. From the
entry through the return delay slot is 24 instructions, below the cap.

`lw` at `0x8007a250` loads the word at `0x10(a0)` into `v1`. That address
is `0x8009f378`. The executable image holds 0 there, so `v1` is 0.

`beq` at `0x8007a254` compares `a1` with zero. `a1` is `1`, so the branch
to `0x8007a288` is not taken. The delay slot at `0x8007a258` is `nop`.

`lw` at `0x8007a25c` loads the word at `0(a0)` into `v0`. That address is
`0x8009f368`. The executable image holds 0 there, so `v0` is 0. The `nop`
at `0x8007a260` follows the load.

`ori` at `0x8007a264` ORs `0x2` into `v0`, so `v0` is `2`.

`beq` at `0x8007a268` compares `v1` with zero. `v1` is 0, so the branch to
`0x8007a27c` is taken. The delay slot at `0x8007a26c` stores `v0` at
`0(a0)`, so `0x8009f368` becomes `2`.

`sw` at `0x8007a27c` stores 0 at `0x1c(a0)`, which is `0x8009f384`.

`j` at `0x8007a280` goes to `0x8007a2a0`. The delay slot at `0x8007a284`
sets `a1` to 0.

`lw` at `0x8007a2a0` loads the word at `0x1c(a0)` into `v0`. That word is
0, so `v0` is 0. `sw` at `0x8007a2a4` stores `a1` at `0x18(a0)`, which is
`0x8009f380`, so that word becomes 0.

`jr` at `0x8007a2a8` returns through `ra`. `ra` stays `0x8007a3b0`. The
delay slot at `0x8007a2ac` stores `v0` at `0x14(a0)`, which is
`0x8009f37c`, so that word becomes 0. `v0` stays 0.

The words at `0x8007a270` are the other side of the `v1` test. This
arrival does not enter them. They load the word at `0x4(v1)`, store it at
`0x1c(a0)`, and jump to `0x8007a280`.

The words at `0x8007a288` are the `a1` zero side. This arrival does not
enter them. They store `v1` at `0x1c(a0)`, set `v1` to `0xfffffffd`, mask
the word at `0(a0)` with that value, copy the word at `0x4(a0)` into `a1`,
and fall into `0x8007a2a0`.

These words do not read `a2` or `a3`. `a2` stays `0x80010db0`. `a3` stays
0. There is no named call. The next address is `0x8007a3b0`.

## Our code

`components/exe/call_8007a250.cpp` updates the four words. When `a1` is
not 0 it sets bit 1 of the first word. A zero link stores 0. A nonzero
link loads the word 4 bytes past that link. When `a1` is 0 it clears bit
1 and copies the link and the word at offset 4. A host check uses the
arrival values and local values for the other two sides. The check does
not run a retail load.
