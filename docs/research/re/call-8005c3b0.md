# Call at 0x8005c3b0

Traced fact from the USA PS-X EXE. Re-check on a disc you own with `python3 tools/entry_disasm.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x8005c3b0`. The executable and the listing stay in `build/`.

The start address is `0x8005c3b0`. [call-8005c254.md](call-8005c254.md) stops on the instruction before this one. `v0` is 0. `a2` is 0. `a3` is 0. `s0` is `0x800e44e0`. `ra` is `0x8005c82c`.
