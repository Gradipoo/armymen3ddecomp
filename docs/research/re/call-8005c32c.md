# Call at 0x8005c32c

Traced fact from the USA PS-X EXE. Re-check on a disc you own with `python3 tools/entry_disasm.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x8005c32c`. The executable and the listing stay in `build/`.

The start address is `0x8005c32c`. [call-8005c348-2.md](call-8005c348-2.md) reaches this shift. The counted bytes are `ATRACKS` and a 0. None of the letters is `0x2e` or `0x3b`. The 0 ends the loop. `a2` is 7. `a3` is 0.

`jal` at `0x8005c3b8` targets `0x80076508`. `ra` becomes `0x8005c3c0`. The delay stores 0 after those seven bytes. The bytes at `sp+0x10` are `ATRACKS`. The earlier notes at `0x80076508` stay. The next note is this arrival.
