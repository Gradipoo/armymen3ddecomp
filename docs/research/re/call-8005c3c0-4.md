Traced fact from the USA PS-X EXE. Re-check on a disc you own with `python3 tools/entry_disasm.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x8005c3c0`. The executable and the listing stay in `build/`.

`sw` at `0x8005c3c4` stores `0xddd69adb` at `0x800e450c`. `v0` becomes `0x800e4510`. `sw` at `0x8005c3cc` stores that value at `0x800d89d4`. `jr` at `0x8005c3d8` returns to `0x8005c668`. That arrival is in [call-8005c668-3.md](call-8005c668-3.md).
