# Block at 0x800882c4

Traced fact from the USA PS-X EXE. Re-check on a disc you own with `python3 tools/entry_disasm.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x800882c4`. The executable and the listing stay in `build/`.

The start address is `0x800882c4`. It is not in `tools/sdk_names.txt`. `python3 tools/sdk_match.py` does not name it. The `jalr` in [call-80088458.md](call-80088458.md) jumps here and sets `ra` to `0x8008846c`. On arrival `a0` is `0x800e160c` and `a1` is 0. There is no branch and no `jal`. The hard cap is 64 instructions. This block ends at `jr ra` before the cap.

`lui` and `ori` write `0x04000002` into `v1`. `lw` loads `0x1f801814` from `0x800b76d0` into `v0`. `sw` stores `0x04000002` at that address. `lw` loads `0x1f8010a0` from `0x800b76d4` into `v0`. `sw` stores `a0` (`0x800e160c`) at that address. `lw` loads `0x1f8010a4` from `0x800b76d8` into `v0`. `sw` stores 0 at that address. `lui` and `ori` write `0x01000401` into `v1`. `lw` loads `0x1f8010a8` from `0x800b76dc` into `v0`. `jr` returns through `ra`. The delay slot stores `0x01000401` at `0x1f8010a8`. `ra` is `0x8008846c`.

After the delay slot, control goes to `0x8008846c`. `a0` stays `0x800e160c`. `a1` stays 0. The word at `0x800b76f8` is still `0xd`. The display x and y halfwords are still not quoted. This note does not close the gap in `components/exe/load.cpp`.
