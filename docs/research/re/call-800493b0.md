No traced entry reaches this address. The prologue saves `s0` and `ra`. `jal` at `0x800493bc` targets `0x80049330` and sets `ra` to `0x800493c4`. The delay slot copies `a1` into `s0`. [call-80049330.md](call-80049330.md) names that target. This note does not enter it. The next instruction compares `v0` with 0. That note does not state `v0` on return, so the words after the delay slot are not traced.

The 20 bytes from `0x800493b0` through `0x800493c0` occur once.
