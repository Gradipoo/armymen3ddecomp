[call-800450d4.md](call-800450d4.md) reaches this address. `a0` is not stated. `ra` would be `0x800450e8`.

The prologue saves `s0` and `ra` and copies `a0` into `s0`. `lw` reads the word at `s0+0x194` into `v1`. That word is not stated. `lw` reads the word at `v1+0x1c` into `v0`. That word is not stated. `lh` reads the halfword at `v0+0x28` into `a0`. `lw` reads the word at `v0+0x2c` into `v0`. `jalr` at `0x80044fc0` jumps through that word. The delay slot adds the halfword to `v1` and writes `a0`. This note stops at that call and does not enter it.

The 52 bytes from `0x80044f94` through `0x80044fc4` also sit at `0x80044f4c`. This note covers only `0x80044f94`.
