# Caller fallthrough at 0x800650ec

The [released path](call-800650e0.md) tests bit `0x40` of complemented
input `a0` at `0x800650e4`. `beq` falls through to `0x800650ec` when that
result is nonzero. The delay slot at `0x800650e8` sets `v0` to `0x7f` on
either side. This note starts at that fallthrough. It does not resume the
parked static boot.

The [clear path](call-800650f4.md) branches to `0x800650f4` when bit
`0x40` is zero and stores zero at `s0+0xb`. This path stores `0x7f` at
that byte in the delay slot of `j` to `0x800650f8` before joining the same
tail.

`python3 tools/boot_slice.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x800650ec --max 128`
reports 19 instructions and stops at `bne` `0x8006518c`. The report stays
in `build/`.

At `0x800650ec`, `j` targets `0x800650f8`. The delay slot at `0x800650f0`
stores the low byte of `v0` at `s0+0xb`, so `0x7f` lands on the record.
`sb` at `0x800650f8` stores zero at `s0+0x13`. `sb` at `0x800650fc` stores
zero at `s0+0x14`. `lw` at `0x80065100` loads `a1` from `sp+0x58`. That
word is not stated.

`jal` at `0x80065104` targets [call-8006471c](call-8006471c.md). The delay
slot copies `s5` into `a0`. `ra` is `0x8006510c`. The named helper body is
not entered here.

After the call, `j` at `0x8006510c` targets `0x8006516c`. The delay slot
at `0x80065110` adds `0x7c` to `s0`. The tail at `0x8006516c` adds four to
`s4`, one to `s5`, shifts `fp` left, and adds one to `s7`. It loads words
from `sp+0x28` and `sp+0x1c`. Their live values are not stated. It adds
four to the `sp+0x28` word and stores it back in the delay slot of the
next branch.

`bne` at `0x8006518c` compares `s7` with the limit from `sp+0x1c`. The
taken target is `0x80064e40`. Fallthrough is `0x80065194`. The limit word
and taken side are unstated. This note stops at that branch. It does not
enter either path.

## Our code

`components/exe/call_800650ec.cpp` stores `0x7f` at offset `0x0b`, clears
bytes at offsets `0x13` and `0x14`, calls `call_8006471c`, advances the
record pointer by `0x7c`, and updates the scan counters through the delay
slot before the branch recorded in [call-8006518c.md](call-8006518c.md).

`menu_input_scan_step` calls this block when `call_800650e0` reports bit
`0x40` set on the complemented halfword from [call_80064f48](call-80064f48.md).

## Gap

The loop branch at `0x8006518c` is recorded in [call-8006518c.md](call-8006518c.md).
Words at `sp+0x58`, `sp+0x28`, and `sp+0x1c` stay unstated. Caller resume at
`0x80065194` and the body at `0x80064e40` are not covered here.
