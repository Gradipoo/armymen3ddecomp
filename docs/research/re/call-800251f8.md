# Call at 0x800251f8

Traced fact from the USA PS-X EXE. Re-check on a disc you own with `python3 tools/entry_disasm.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x800251f8`. The executable and the listing stay in `build/`.

The start address is `0x800251f8`. The `jal` at `0x8007fae4` set `ra` to `0x8007faec` and jumped here. That call is in [entry-after-third-compare.md](entry-after-third-compare.md). The tool followed 11 instructions and stopped at the first jump. The hard cap is 64 instructions. This block ended before the cap.

`addiu` at `0x800251f8` subtracts `0x50` from `sp`. Stores from `0x800251fc` through `0x8002521c` write `ra`, `fp`, `s7`, `s6`, `s5`, `s4`, `s3`, `s2`, and `s1` at offsets `0x4c`, `0x48`, `0x44`, `0x40`, `0x3c`, `0x38`, `0x34`, `0x30`, and `0x2c` from that `sp`. The store at `0x800251fc` saves the incoming `ra`, which is `0x8007faec`.

`jal` at `0x80025220` targets `0x8007faf0` and sets `ra` to `0x80025228`. The delay slot at `0x80025224` is `sw s0, 0x28(sp)`. Re-check it with `--start 0x80025224`. After the delay slot, control goes to `0x8007faf0`. The saved registers do not change that target. The only branch or jump in this block is that `jal`.

This block writes `sp`, `ra`, and ten words on the stack: the incoming `ra`, `fp`, `s7` through `s1`, and `s0` in the delay slot. It reads those registers and `sp`. It does not read `a0`, `v0`, `t0`, or the word at `0x8007f9a8`. The callee is the block in [call-8007faf0.md](call-8007faf0.md). The return continues in [call-80025228.md](call-80025228.md). The caller return at `0x8007faec` is not traced. This note does not close the gap in `components/exe/load.cpp`.
