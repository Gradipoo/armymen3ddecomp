# Eighth arrival at 0x8007a668

Traced fact from the USA PS-X EXE. Re-check on a disc you own with `python3 tools/entry_disasm.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x8007a668`. The executable and the listing stay in `build/`.

The start address is `0x8007a668`. The `jr` in [call-8007a570-26.md](call-8007a570-26.md) returns here. `v0` is `0x8001003c`. `s0` is `0x8001001c`. `s1` is 8. `s2` is `0x8001007c`. The frame at `0x800251f8` stays open. Its saved `ra` stays `0x8007faec`.

`addu` at `0x8007a668` copies `v0` into `a0`, so `a0` is `0x8001003c`. `bne` at `0x8007a66c` is taken. `beq` at `0x8007a680` is not taken, because `a0` and `s0` differ.

Control goes to `0x8007a688`. That block is the next note. The caller return at `0x8007faec` is not traced.
