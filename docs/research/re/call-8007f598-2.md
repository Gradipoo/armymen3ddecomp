The delay of the third sets `t0` to 7. `slt` leaves `at` at 1, because 7 is below `0x11`. `beq` at `0x8007f5b4` is not taken. The low 2 bits of `a0` are 0, so `beq` at `0x8007f5bc` is taken. The byte loop at `0x8007f5cc` does not run.

This note does not quote those bytes. After the fourth pass `a0` is `0x800d89ec` and `a1` is `0x800e581d`.

`a2` is 1, so the byte loop at `0x8007f640` runs once. It copies the byte at `0x800e581d` to `0x800d89ec`. `a2` becomes 0. `jr` at `0x8007f658` returns through `ra`. The delay slot is `nop`. `v0` stays `0x800d89dc`. `ra` leaves as `0x8005c824`. The return is not traced.
