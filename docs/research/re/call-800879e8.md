# Block at 0x800879e8

Traced fact from the USA PS-X EXE. Re-check on a disc you own with `python3 tools/entry_disasm.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x800879e8`. The executable and the listing stay in `build/`.

The start address is `0x800879e8`. It is not in `tools/sdk_names.txt`. `python3 tools/sdk_match.py` does not name it. The `jal` in [call-80087698.md](call-80087698.md) calls here and sets `ra` to `0x800876a4`. On arrival `a0` is `0x800e15fc`. [call-8008aafc.md](call-8008aafc.md) stored halfword 0 at that address, halfword 0 at `0x800e15fe`, halfword 0 at `0x800e1600`, and halfword 0 at `0x800e1602`. `a0` is not zero, so `bne` is taken. The tool stopped at that branch. This note follows the taken side and stops at `jr ra`. The hard cap is 64 instructions. This block ended before the cap.

`bne` at `0x800879e8` branches to `0x800879f8`. The delay slot opens a frame of `0x10`. The fall-through at `0x800879f0` is not taken.

`lbu` at `0x800879f8` loads the byte at `0x800e15fc`. It is 0. `srl` by 3 leaves `a1` at 0, and `sw` stores that 0 at `0x0(sp)`. `lh` loads the halfword at `0x800e1600`. It is 0. Subtracting it from zero, keeping the low 8 bits, and shifting right by 3 leaves `a2` at 0. `sw` stores that 0 at `0x8(sp)`. `lbu` loads the byte at `0x800e15fe`. It is 0. Shifting `a1` left by 10 leaves `a1` at 0. Shifting the loaded byte right by 3 leaves `v0` at 0. `sw` stores that 0 at `0x4(sp)`. Shifting `v0` left by 15 leaves `v0` at 0. `lh` loads the halfword at `0x800e1602`. It is 0. `lui` writes `0xe2000000` into `a0`. `or` joins `a0` into `a1`, so `a1` is `0xe2000000`. `or` joins that into `v0`, so `v0` is `0xe2000000`. Subtracting the loaded halfword from zero, keeping the low 8 bits, and shifting right by 3 leaves `v1` at 0. `sll` of that by 5 writes 0 into `a0`. The next two `or` instructions join 0 into `v0`, so `v0` stays `0xe2000000`. `sw` stores 0 at `0xc(sp)`.

`jr` returns through `ra`. The delay slot closes the frame, so those four stack stores do not leave this call. `ra` is `0x800876a4`. `v0` is `0xe2000000`. `a0`, `a1`, `a2`, and `v1` are 0.
