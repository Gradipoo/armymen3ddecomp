That tool stops at `jr ra`. Re-check the delay slot with `--start 0x80010ce0`. That run's first instruction is the `addiu`. The words after that instruction are not traced.

This follows [call-80010cc4.md](call-80010cc4.md). It is the return from the `jal` at `0x80010cc8`. The function entry stays `0x80010c90`. See [call-80010c90.md](call-80010c90.md). Before that `jal`, the frame is open, `s0` is the object, and `s1` is the second argument. `sp` is `0x20` below the value at the function entry. The slot at `0x10(sp)` holds the old `s0`. The slot at `0x14(sp)` holds the old `s1`. The slot at `0x18(sp)` holds the original `ra`. That saved `ra` is the caller of `0x80010c90`. Its value is not known. [call-8002fc2c.md](call-8002fc2c.md) traces the callee only through its first unnamed `jal`. That prefix subtracts `0x20` from `sp` and does not read this frame. That note stops before a return. This note does not treat those three slots as the arriving words. This note covers 5 instructions, including the delay slot, and ends at `jr ra`. The hard cap is 64 instructions. There is no `jal` or `jalr`, so there is no named SDK or BIOS call.

`lw` at `0x80010cd0` loads the word at `0x18(sp)` into `ra`. `lw` at `0x80010cd4` loads the word at `0x14(sp)` into `s1`. That load fills the delay of the `ra` load, and it does not read `ra`. `lw` at `0x80010cd8` loads the word at `0x10(sp)` into `s0`. That load fills the delay of the `s1` load, and it does not read `s1`. `jr` at `0x80010cdc` returns through the loaded `ra`. The `s0` load delay is this jump, and the jump does not read `s0`. The delay slot at `0x80010ce0` is `addiu sp, sp, 0x20`. The three loads use `sp` before that add.

This block writes `ra`, `s1`, `s0`, and `sp`. It reads `sp` and the three words at `0x10(sp)`, `0x14(sp)`, and `0x18(sp)`. It does not read or write `v0`, `a0`, `a1`, `a2`, or `a3`. `v0` stays as the callee left it. That value is not known. `ra` leaves as the word loaded from `0x18(sp)`. `s1` leaves as the word loaded from `0x14(sp)`. `s0` leaves as the word loaded from `0x10(sp)`. `sp` is `0x20` above the arrival value.

After the delay slot, control goes to the loaded `ra`. The next address is that word. The words at `0x80010ce4` are not traced. `0x80010ce4` is inside the text image, `0x80010000` through `0x800c57ff`. This note does not close the gap in `components/exe/load.cpp`.
