# Return at 0x8007a570 and the repeated cursors

Traced fact from the USA PS-X EXE. Re-check on a disc you own with `python3 tools/entry_disasm.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x8007a570`. The executable and the listing stay in `build/`.

The start address is `0x8007a570`. [call-8007a5a4-57.md](call-8007a5a4-57.md) leaves `s2` here at `0x80010048`. `s3` is 0. `s4` is `0x8001007c`. The caller `s0` is `0x80010044` and the caller `s1` is 18. The frame at `0x800251f8` stays open. Its saved `ra` stays `0x8007faec`.

Cursor `0x80010048` matches [call-8007a570-46.md](call-8007a570-46.md) and [call-8007a5a4-52.md](call-8007a5a4-52.md), so `s2` becomes `0x8001004c`. Cursor `0x8001004c` matches [call-8007a570-47.md](call-8007a570-47.md) and [call-8007a5a4-54.md](call-8007a5a4-54.md), so `s2` becomes `0x80010050`. Cursor `0x80010050` matches [call-8007a570-48.md](call-8007a570-48.md) and [call-8007a5a4-55.md](call-8007a5a4-55.md), so `s2` becomes `0x80010054`. This note does not disassemble `0x8007a4e4` again.

The live word at `0x80010054` is `0x800b3b8c`, from [call-8007a668-17.md](call-8007a668-17.md). [call-8007a570-49.md](call-8007a570-49.md) saw `0x800b5d60` there. The next lap is the next note. The caller return at `0x8007faec` is not traced.
