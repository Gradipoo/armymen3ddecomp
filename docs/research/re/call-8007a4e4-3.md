# Third call at 0x8007a4e4

Traced fact from the USA PS-X EXE. Re-check on a disc you own with `python3 tools/entry_disasm.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x8007a4e4`. The executable and the listing stay in `build/`.

The start address is `0x8007a4e4`. The `jal` in [call-8007a540-3.md](call-8007a540-3.md) jumps here. `a0` is `0x8005bf80`. `ra` is `0x8007a5a4`. The frame at `0x800251f8` stays open. Its saved `ra` stays `0x8007faec`.

`a1` starts at `0x80010000` and `a3` is `0x8001007c`, the same words as [call-8007a4e4.md](call-8007a4e4.md). The loop reads the live pointer words. [call-8007a688.md](call-8007a688.md) stored `0x800b5948` at `0x80010004` and `0x800b3b68` at `0x8001000c`. The file bytes at those two slots are not the live words.

The word at `0x80010000` is `0x800b3b00`, and the word there is `0x80059bc8`. The live word at `0x80010004` is `0x800b5948`, and the word there is `0x80062194`. The word at `0x80010008` is `0x800b3b8c`, and the word there is `0x8005c874`. The live word at `0x8001000c` is `0x800b3b68`, and the word there is `0x8005bf80`. That matches `a0`. `a2` becomes `0x800b3b68`. `beq` at `0x8007a530` is not taken after that copy.

`jr` at `0x8007a538` returns through `ra`. The delay slot copies `a2` into `v0`. `v0` leaves as `0x800b3b68`. `ra` leaves as `0x8007a5a4`. [call-8007a4e4-2.md](call-8007a4e4-2.md) returned the same pointer from the earlier slot at `0x80010004`. This call finds it at `0x8001000c`.

Control goes to `0x8007a5a4`. The caller return at `0x8007faec` is not traced.
