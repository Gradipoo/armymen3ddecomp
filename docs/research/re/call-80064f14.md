# Caller continuation at 0x80064f14

The [previous capture](call-80064e58.md) measured this indirect destination
after named PadGetState. This is another partial caller block. It does
not identify a complete input function or resume the parked static boot.
Prepared reports and capture evidence stay local under
`build/coordination/menu-button-branch/`.

At `0x80064f14`, the caller loads an unsigned byte through `s1`, followed
by a load-delay nop. The bne at `0x80064f1c` takes `0x80065144` if the
loaded byte is nonzero. Its delay slot increments `s1` by one.

The principal skipped Green Plastic News with Return held for 200 ms,
observed NEW GAME, opened the measurement gate, pressed Down for 100 ms,
and observed OPTIONS. The same local emulator, BIOS, full boot,
interpreter, owner disc and initial card copies described in the previous
record were used. This run does not establish the exact guest input edge.

All 600 branch samples, frames 4247 through 4846, had `v0=0`,
`s1=0x800e18d8`, and `s2=2`. Thus these samples fall through to
`0x80064f24`, with the delay slot advancing `s1` to `0x800e18d9`.
The hook stopped at frame 4847 with zero errors and overflow. The process
was interrupted afterward, exit 255. No meaning is assigned to this byte.

A prepared slice at the measured fallthrough loads the next unsigned
byte, waits through a nop, masks it to eight bits into `a2`, and compares
it with `0x41`. The beq at `0x80064f34` takes `0x80064f48` on equality;
fallthrough is `0x80064f3c`. Its delay slot increments `s1` again.
The report stops at that unknown branch. The second byte was not captured
and the next path is not guessed.

This observation resolves one branch. It does not establish button bits,
selection state, or a native menu update. No function status was promoted.
