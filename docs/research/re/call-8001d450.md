# Block at 0x8001d450

Traced fact from the USA PS-X EXE. Re-check on a disc you own with `python3 tools/entry_disasm.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x8001d450`. The executable and the listing stay in `build/`. That tool stops at the first branch, the `bne` at `0x8001d464`. Re-check the delay slot with `--start 0x8001d468`. That run's first instruction is the `nop`. The words after that instruction are not traced.

The start address is `0x8001d450`. This is the return from the `jal` in [call-8001d434.md](call-8001d434.md). That `jal` targets `tolower` at `0x8007ff80` and sets `ra` to here. This note does not enter that body. On arrival `s0` is the pointer to the byte just passed to `tolower`. The frame from `0x8001d434` is still open. The slot at `0x10(sp)` holds the old `s0`. The slot at `0x14(sp)` holds the saved `ra`, which is `0x80010d7c`. `v0` is the value left by `tolower`. This note does not state that number. The tool followed 6 instructions and stopped at the `bne`. This note covers 7 instructions, including the delay slot, and stops at that unknown branch. The hard cap is 64 instructions. There is no `jal`, `jalr`, or `jr` in this span.

`sb` at `0x8001d450` stores the low 8 bits of `v0` at `0(s0)`. `addiu` at `0x8001d454` adds 1 to `s0`. `lbu` at `0x8001d458` loads the unsigned byte at `0(s0)` into `a0`. The `nop` at `0x8001d45c` fills the load delay. `sll` at `0x8001d460` shifts that byte left by 24 into `a0`. The low 24 bits are clear, so `a0` is zero only when the byte is zero.

`bne` at `0x8001d464` compares `a0` with zero and goes to `0x8001d448` when the byte is not zero. The byte is not known, so this note does not know which side is taken. The delay slot at `0x8001d468` is `nop`.

This block writes the byte at `0(s0)` before the increment, then `s0` and `a0`. It reads `v0`, `s0`, and the next byte. It does not read `sp`. It does not write `v0` or `sp`. It does not load the saved `ra`. `v0` stays the value left by `tolower`. `s0` leaves one byte past the stored byte. This note does not state the register `ra` after the unentered call. The saved slot stays `0x80010d7c`.

The words at `0x8001d448` are not traced. The words at `0x8001d46c` are not traced. Both addresses are inside the text image, `0x80010000` through `0x800c57ff`. This note does not close the gap in `components/exe/load.cpp`.
