No traced entry reaches this address. `addiu` writes `0xb0` into `t2`. `jr` jumps through `t2`. The delay slot writes `0xb` into `t1`. This is the B table. [bios.md](bios.md) does not name that number. This note does not enter the table.

The 12 bytes from `0x800901ac` through the delay slot occur once.
