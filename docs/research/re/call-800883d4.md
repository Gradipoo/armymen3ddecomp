# Block at 0x800883d4

Traced fact from the USA PS-X EXE. Re-check on a disc you own with `python3 tools/entry_disasm.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x800883d4`. The executable and the listing stay in `build/`.

The start address is `0x800883d4`. The `jal` in [call-800883c4.md](call-800883c4.md) calls `SetIntrMask` and returns here. On arrival `a0` is 0. `s0` is `0x800e160c`. `s1` is `0x40`. `s2` is 0. `s3` is `0x80082048`. `v0` is the `SetIntrMask` result. This note does not quote that result. The byte at `0x800b75c9` is not stored in these notes, so the `beq` side is unknown. This note stops there. The hard cap is 64 instructions. This block ended before the cap.

`lui` and `addiu` set `a0` to `0x800b75c8`. `sw` stores `v0` at `0x800b76f8`. `lbu` loads the byte at `0x1(a0)`, which is `0x800b75c9`, into `v1`. This note does not quote that byte. `addiu` sets `v0` to 1. `beq` at `0x800883ec` branches to `0x80088440` when `v1` is 0. The delay slot stores `v0` at `0x8(a0)`, so the word at `0x800b75d0` becomes 1. The fall-through is `0x800883f4`. Which side this boot takes is unknown.

After the delay slot, the word at `0x800b76f8` is the `SetIntrMask` result. The word at `0x800b75d0` is 1. `s0`, `s1`, `s2`, and `s3` stay as they arrived. The words at `0x800883f4` and `0x80088440` are not traced. The display x and y halfwords are still not quoted. This note does not close the gap in `components/exe/load.cpp`.
