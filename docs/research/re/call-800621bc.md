# Block at 0x800621bc

Traced fact from the USA PS-X EXE. Re-check on a disc you own with `python3 tools/entry_disasm.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x800621bc`. The executable and the listing stay in `build/`.

The start address is `0x800621bc`. It is the return address of the `jal` in [call-80062194.md](call-80062194.md). This note does not use the `v0` from `0x8007f920`. `s0` is `0x800e0000`. `s1` is `0x800dd488`. The saved `ra` at `0x18(sp)` is `0x8007a6bc`. The frame at `0x800251f8` stays open. Its saved `ra` stays `0x8007faec`.

`addiu` at `0x800621bc` sets `v0` to `0xffffffff`. `sw` at `0x800621c0` stores that word at `0x800dd488`. `sw` at `0x800621c4` stores the same word at `0x800dd544`, which is `0xbc` past `s1`. `lw` at `0x800621c8` restores `ra`. `lw` at `0x800621cc` restores `s1`. `lw` at `0x800621d0` restores `s0`. `jr` at `0x800621d4` returns through `ra`. The delay slot adds `0x20` to `sp`.

`ra` leaves as `0x8007a6bc`. `v0` leaves as `0xffffffff`. The caller `s0` is still `0x80010004` and the caller `s1` is 3, from the delay slot at `0x8007a6b0`. The caller `s2` is still `0x8001007c`. Control goes to `0x8007a6bc`. The first arrival there is already noted. This arrival is not. The caller return at `0x8007faec` is not traced.
