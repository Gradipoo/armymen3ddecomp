# Loop fallthrough at 0x80065194

[call-8006518c.md](call-8006518c.md) records fallthrough from the loop
compare when `s7` is not signed-less-than the limit at `sp+0x1c`. That path
lands here. The [Up path](call-800650d0.md), [0x40 set path](call-800650ec.md),
and [cleared 0x40 path](call-800650f4.md) reach this address after the same
shared tail. This note does not resume the parked static boot.

`python3 tools/boot_slice.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x80065194 --max 64`
reports two instructions and stops at an unknown branch. The report stays
in `build/`.

| Address | Instruction |
| --- | --- |
| `0x80065194` | `bne s5, zero, 0x800651c8` |
| `0x80065198` | `lui v0, 0x800e` |

`bne` at `0x80065194` compares retail `s5` with zero. The taken target is
`0x800651c8`. The fallthrough is `0x8006519c`. The delay slot at
`0x80065198` sets the upper half of `v0` to `0x800e` on the not-taken side.
Live `s5` after the tail increment in [call-800650d0.md](call-800650d0.md)
was zero before the helper call in the Up capture, then one after the tail
at `0x8006516c`. That observation does not state the side at this `bne`.
This note does not enter the body at `0x800651c8`.

The slice contains no `jal`.

## Our code

`components/exe/call_80065194.cpp` follows the not-taken side when `s5` is
zero and records `v0` as `0x800e0000`. When `s5` is nonzero it records the
taken target without entering `0x800651c8`. Portable glue passes
`MenuInputScanState::index` as `s5`, matching the word incremented in the
d0, ec, and f4 tails and passed as `a0` to [call-8006471c](call-8006471c.md).

`menu_input_scan_step` calls this block when a tail sets `loop_again` false
from `menu_scan_should_loop` in [call-8006518c.md](call-8006518c.md).
`run_menu_input_scan_frames` inherits that result on the last step.

## Gap

The body at `0x800651c8` is not entered. Instructions after `0x80065198` on
the zero-`s5` fallthrough are not traced. The delay-slot store of `t0` at
`sp+0x28` from [call-8006518c.md](call-8006518c.md) is not modeled. Live
`s5` on retail when the loop limit stops the scan is not stated.
