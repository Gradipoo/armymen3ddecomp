# Paths at 0x8002523c and 0x8002524c

Traced fact from the USA PS-X EXE. Re-check on a disc you own with `python3 tools/entry_disasm.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x8002523c` and with `--start 0x8002524c`. The executable and the listing stay in `build/`.

Both starts follow the `beq` in [call-80025228.md](call-80025228.md). That branch sends control to `0x8002524c` when the word at `0x8007f9a8` is 0, and to `0x8002523c` when it is not. The delay slot at `0x80025238` has already set `v1` to `0x8007f9a8` on both paths. Which value this boot has is still unknown, because that word ORs BIOS byte-compare results and the BIOS ROM is not in this tree.

From `0x8002523c` the tool followed 9 instructions and stopped at the first jump. The hard cap is 64 instructions. This path ended before the cap. `lw` at `0x8002523c` reads `0x28(v1)` into `v0`, the word at `0x8007f9d0`. `nop` at `0x80025240` sits between that load and the add. `addiu` at `0x80025244` adds `0x1400` to `v0`, and `sw` at `0x80025248` stores the sum back at `0x8007f9d0`. Those four words are not a branch, so control falls through to `0x8002524c`.

From `0x8002524c` the tool followed 5 instructions and stopped at the first jump. The hard cap is 64 instructions. This path ended before the cap. `lui` at `0x8002524c` loads `0x800a0000` into `s0`. `addiu` at `0x80025250` adds `-0x7e0`, so `s1` is `0x8009f820`. `addu` at `0x80025254` copies `s1` into `a0`, and `addu` at `0x80025258` writes 0 into `a1`. The non-zero path runs these same four words after its store.

`jal` at `0x8002525c` targets `0x8007f920` and sets `ra` to `0x80025264`. The delay slot at `0x80025260` is `addiu a2, zero, 0x9a4`, so `a2` is `0x9a4`. Re-check it with `--start 0x80025260`. That run's first instruction is the `addiu`. The words after that instruction are not traced. After the delay slot, both paths go to `0x8007f920`. The argument registers do not change that target. The only branch or jump on either path is that `jal`.

The non-zero path writes `v0` and the word at `0x8007f9d0`. Both paths then write `s0`, `s1`, `a0`, `a1`, `ra`, and `a2` in the delay slot. They read `v1` and, on the non-zero path, the word at `0x8007f9d0`. They do not read or write `sp` or the stack slots saved at `0x800251f8`. The first block at `0x8007f920` is in [call-8007f920.md](call-8007f920.md). The words at `0x80025264` are not traced. The caller return at `0x8007faec` is not traced. This note does not close the gap in `components/exe/load.cpp`.
