# Nineteenth entry at 0x80076508

Traced fact from the USA PS-X EXE. Re-check on a disc you own with `python3 tools/entry_disasm.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x80076508`. The executable and the listing stay in `build/`.

The start address is `0x80076508`. The `jal` in [call-8005c3b0.md](call-8005c3b0.md) jumps here. `a0` is `sp+0x10` from that caller. The byte there is 0. `ra` is `0x8005c3c0`. The earlier notes at this address stay.

`s2` becomes 0. `lbu` at `0x80076528` loads 0. `beq` at `0x80076530` is taken. The delay slot clears `s0`. The `tolower` call does not run. `addu` at `0x80076588` copies `s2` into `v0`, so `v0` is 0. `jr` at `0x800765a0` returns through `ra`. The delay slot adds `0x28` to `sp`. Control goes to `0x8005c3c0`.
