# Loop re-entry at 0x80064e40

The [Up path](call-800650d0.md) ends at `bne` `0x8006518c` with taken
target `0x80064e40`. This note starts there. It is a partial caller
block. It does not resume the parked static boot at `0x80061fe4`.

`python3 tools/boot_slice.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x80064e40 --max 128`
reports ten instructions and stops at an unknown branch. The report stays
in `build/`.

At `0x80064e40`, `lw` loads `t0` from `sp+0x18`. That word is not stated.
`addu` at `0x80064e44` clears `s6`. `sll` at `0x80064e48` shifts `t0` left
by four. `or` at `0x80064e4c` stores `v0 | s7` in `s3`.

`jal` at `0x80064e50` targets `0x80094fe0`. `sdk_match` names that target
`PadGetState`. The delay slot at `0x80064e54` copies `s3` into `a0`. `ra`
is `0x80064e58`. The SDK body is not entered.

At the return, `addu` copies `v0` into `s2`. `sltiu` at `0x80064e5c` sets
`v0` to one when `s2` is unsigned less than seven, otherwise zero. `beq`
at `0x80064e60` compares that result with zero. The delay slot at
`0x80064e64` loads `0x800c` into the high half of `v0`. The taken target
is `0x80064f14`. Fallthrough is `0x80064e68`. Static boot cannot state
the side. This note stops at that branch.

[call-80064e58.md](call-80064e58.md) already records this return and a
later capture where sampled `s2` was two, so those samples fell through.
This slice does not treat that capture as the side of a new instruction.

## Our code

`components/exe/call_80064e40.cpp` clears `s6`, builds the `PadGetState`
argument `(sp+0x18 << 4) | s7`, records the return in `s2`, and records
whether that return is unsigned less than seven. It does not take the
branch.

## Gap

The branch at `0x80064e60` is unstated in this slice. The word at
`sp+0x18` stays unstated. The `PadGetState` body is not covered. The
fallthrough and the taken target stay in the earlier notes.

The fallthrough block is [call-80064e68.md](call-80064e68.md).
