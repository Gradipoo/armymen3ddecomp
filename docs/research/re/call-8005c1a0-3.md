# Third entry at 0x8005c1a0

Traced fact from the USA PS-X EXE. Re-check on a disc you own with `python3 tools/entry_disasm.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x8005c1a0`. The executable and the listing stay in `build/`.

The start address is `0x8005c1a0`. The `jal` in [call-8005c514.md](call-8005c514.md) jumps here. `a0` is `0x800e54e0`. `a1` is `0x16`. `a2` is 0. `ra` is `0x8005c5e8`. The earlier notes at this address stay.

`s1` becomes `0x800e54e0`. `a0` becomes `0x16`. `a1` becomes `sp+0x10`. `jal` at `0x8005c1bc` targets `0x80081ec8`. `tools/sdk_names.txt` names that address `CdIntToPos`. `ra` becomes `0x8005c1c4`. The delay slot copies `a2` into `s0`, so `s0` is 0. This note does not enter that body. The return is the next note.
