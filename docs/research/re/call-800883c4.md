# Block at 0x800883c4

Traced fact from the USA PS-X EXE. Re-check on a disc you own with `python3 tools/entry_disasm.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x800883c4`. The executable and the listing stay in `build/`.

The start address is `0x800883c4`. [call-8008838c.md](call-8008838c.md) reaches this `beq` and stops. On a DuckStation fast boot of the USA cue, the first stop at this PC is on that path: `ra` is `0x8008838c`, `s0` is `0x800e160c`, `s1` is `0x40`, and `s2` is 0. `s3` is `0x80082048`. The word at `0x800b76f0` is 0. The word at `0x800b76f4` is 0. `v0` is 1, which is the low 6 bits of that first word plus 1. `v1` is 0. Those two values are not equal, so the `beq` falls through. The same two words, the same registers, and the same side were still there about three seconds later, and on the next stop after that. The hard cap is 64 instructions. This block ended before the cap.

`beq` at `0x800883c4` would branch to `0x80088394` if `v0` equaled `v1`. This boot does not take that side. The delay slot is `nop`. Fall-through is `0x800883cc`. `jal` there targets `0x80085848` and sets `ra` to `0x800883d4`. `python3 tools/sdk_match.py` names that address `SetIntrMask`. The delay slot is `addu a0, zero, zero`, so `a0` becomes 0. At the `beq`, before that delay slot, `a1` is `0x1f801110`, `a2` is `0x40`, and `a3` is 0. This note does not enter `SetIntrMask`.

After the delay slot, control goes to `SetIntrMask`. `a0` is 0. `a1` is `0x1f801110`. `a2` is `0x40`. `a3` is 0. `v0` is 1. `v1` is 0. `s0` stays `0x800e160c`. `s1` stays `0x40`. `s2` stays 0. `s3` stays `0x80082048`. `ra` is `0x800883d4`. The words at `0x800883d4` are not traced. The display x and y halfwords are still not quoted. This note does not close the gap in `components/exe/load.cpp`.
