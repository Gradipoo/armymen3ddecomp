# Twenty-fourth entry at 0x80076508

Traced fact from the USA PS-X EXE. Re-check on a disc you own with `python3 tools/entry_disasm.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x80076508`. The executable and the listing stay in `build/`.

The start address is `0x80076508`. The `jal` in [call-8005c3b8.md](call-8005c3b8.md) jumps here. The first byte is `0x4d`. `ra` is `0x8005c3c0`. The earlier notes at this address stay.

`s2` becomes 0. `beq` at `0x80076530` is not taken. `s0` becomes 0. `s3` becomes `0x800b5bf4`. `jal` at `0x80076540` calls `tolower` with `a0` `0x4d`. `ra` becomes `0x80076548`. This note does not enter `tolower`. The return is the next note.
