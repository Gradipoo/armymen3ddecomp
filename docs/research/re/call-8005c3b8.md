# Call at 0x8005c3b8

Traced fact from the USA PS-X EXE. Re-check on a disc you own with `python3 tools/entry_disasm.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x8005c3b8`. The executable and the listing stay in `build/`.

The start address is `0x8005c3b8`. [call-8005c348-3.md](call-8005c348-3.md) reaches this call. The six name bytes are `MOVIES`. `a2` is 6. `a3` is 0.

`jal` targets `0x80076508`. `ra` becomes `0x8005c3c0`. The delay stores 0 after those six bytes. The bytes at `sp+0x10` are `MOVIES`. The earlier notes at `0x80076508` stay. The arrival is in [call-80076508-24.md](call-80076508-24.md).
