# Caller Up path at 0x800650d0

The [released-input record](call-80064f24.md) followed every complemented
bit test through `0x800650d0` with zero results and took the path at
`0x800650e0`. During the measured Up action from OPTIONS to NEW GAME,
samples split at `0x800650d0`. That observation means `a0 & 0x10` was
nonzero, so this note covers the fallthrough at `0x800650d8`, not
`0x800650e0`. This is a partial caller block. It does not resume the
parked static boot.

The test begins at `0x800650cc` with `andi` on complemented input `a0`
and mask `0x10`. The `beq` at `0x800650d0` compares that result with zero.
The delay slot at `0x800650d4` sets `v0` to `0xffffff80`. On the observed
Up fallthrough, execution continues at `0x800650d8` while the delay slot
still runs.

At `0x800650d8`, `j` targets `0x800650f8`. The delay slot at `0x800650dc`
stores the low byte of `v0` at `s0+0xb`, address offset `0x0b` on the
record base `s0`. After the delay slot, `v0` is `0x80`. The block clears
bytes at offsets `0x13` and `0x14` on the same record. It loads `a1` from
`sp+0x58`. That word is not stated.

`jal` at `0x80065104` targets [call-8006471c](call-8006471c.md). The delay
slot copies `s5` into `a0`. In the Up capture, `s5` was zero. `ra` is
`0x8006510c`. The named helper body is not entered here.

After the call, `j` at `0x8006510c` targets `0x8006516c`. The delay slot
at `0x80065110` adds `0x7c` to `s0`. The tail at `0x8006516c` adds four to
`s4`, one to `s5`, shifts `fp` left, and adds one to `s7`. It loads words
from `sp+0x28` and `sp+0x1c`. Their live values are not stated. It adds
four to the `sp+0x28` word and stores it back in the delay slot of the
next branch.

`bne` at `0x8006518c` compares `s7` with the limit from `sp+0x1c`. The
taken target is `0x80064e40`. Fallthrough is `0x80065194`. The limit word
and taken side are unstated. This note stops at that branch. It does not
enter either path.

In the Up observation, `s0` was `0x800dd748` before the `0x7c` advance.
This block does not identify a menu selection index by itself. It records
how a set `0x10` complemented bit mutates the record and advances the scan
state before the unnamed loop head.

## Our code

`components/exe/call_800650d0.cpp` applies the `0x10` test and, when it
matches, performs the stores, calls `call_8006471c`, advances the record
pointer by `0x7c`, and updates the scan counters through the delay slot
before the branch recorded in [call-8006518c.md](call-8006518c.md).

## Gap

The released path at `0x800650e0` is recorded in
[call-800650e0.md](call-800650e0.md). It stops at the unknown `0x40`
test. The loop branch at `0x8006518c` is recorded in
[call-8006518c.md](call-8006518c.md). It loops to `0x80064e40` when `s7`
is signed-less-than the word at `sp+0x1c`. That word stays unstated.
Words at `sp+0x58` and `sp+0x28` stay unstated. Caller resume at
`0x80065194` and the body at `0x80064e40` are not covered here.

The taken target is recorded in [call-80064e40.md](call-80064e40.md). That
block clears `s6`, builds the `PadGetState` port from `sp+0x18` and `s7`,
and stops at the unknown `beq` at `0x80064e60`. It does not enter either
side of this `bne`.
