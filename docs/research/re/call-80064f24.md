# Caller continuation at 0x80064f24

The [previous block](call-80064f14.md) left the comparison at
`0x80064f34` unknown. This record follows measured register values through
the same caller. The released-input path covers 50 instructions from
`0x80064f24` through the first unnamed call and its delay slot. The note
does not enter that callee. Prepared slices, register traces, scripts,
hashes and images stay under `build/coordination/menu-byte-41/`.

## Comparison and halfword

The caller reads an unsigned byte through `s1`, masks it into `a2`, and
compares it with `0x41` at `0x80064f34`. The delay slot advances `s1`.
All 600 initial samples had `a2=v0=0x41`, so the sampled branch takes
`0x80064f48`. At the comparison, `s1=0x800e18d9`. The hook finished at
frame 5098 with zero overflow.

The taken block updates a stack word by OR with `fp`, then loads an
unsigned halfword through the advanced `s1` into `a1`. It advances `s1`
by two. It loads the word at `0x800dd730` and compares it with four at
`0x80064f70`. The delay slot computes the 32-bit complement of `a1`
into `a0`.

A second 600-sample window found the compared word zero and `t1=4` in
every sample, so the branch takes `0x80064f8c`. There were 594 samples
with `a1=0xffff` and six with `a1=0xffef`. During this window the principal
pressed Up for 100 ms and observed OPTIONS change to NEW GAME. Thus the
changed halfword correlates with that Up action. Its complemented low
16 bits are `0x0010`; the released samples produce zero. The exact guest
edge was not timestamped. This does not assign meanings to other bits.

## Derived masks

At `0x80064f8c`, let `old` be the unsigned halfword at `s0+4`, `raw` be
the loaded halfword, and `new=(~raw)&0xffff`. The code stores:

| Offset from s0 | Stored low 16 bits |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | new |
| 6 | (old XOR new) AND new |
| 8 | (old XOR new) AND raw |

The arithmetic describes current bits, newly set bits and newly cleared
bits. It does not identify an application-level selection field. In these
samples `s0=0x800dd748`. The stores were reviewed in the source slice;
their memory results were not directly captured.

The bne at `0x80064fa8` compares unchanged `a2=0x41` with `0x73`, so it
takes `0x8006508c`. Its delay slot performs the offset-six store. The
next branch tests `s6`, which remains zero through the reviewed path.
The fallthrough stores one at `s0`, then tests complemented input bits
`0x80`, `0x20`, `0x10` and `0x40`. Only the released samples are followed
through all four zero tests here. The Up samples split at the `0x10`
test at `0x800650d0`; that alternate block is not covered by this note.

The released path clears bytes at offsets `0x0a`, `0x0b`, `0x13` and
`0x14`. It loads `a1` from `sp+0x58`, then calls unnamed `0x8006471c`
at `0x80065104`. The delay slot sets `a0=s5`, zero in the measured
samples. The loaded argument at `sp+0x58` is not stated. Return address
is `0x8006510c`. The caller record stops at this unnamed call.

## Observation limits

The principal skipped the intro with Return held for 200 ms, observed
NEW GAME, opened the initial gate, pressed Down for 100 ms and observed
OPTIONS. Retained frames 4440 and 5040 show those selections. The later
Up transition is a separate action. Identity and card-state limits from
the [earlier capture](call-80064e58.md) still apply.

The local hook controller removed each hook after a bounded window and
accepted an explicit next observation without rebooting. A third window
at `0x8006508c` had only three hits, all `s6=0`, during frames 7772 through
8221. The display later showed LOADING and then LOADING DEMO. This sparse
window is not evidence of uninterrupted menu execution. A later Down
press was followed by loading, not an observed menu selection change.

A follow-up hook in the unnamed callee hit 8,768 times in its window.
It retained 600 samples and omitted 8,168. That capture cannot characterize
the full window or establish selection behavior. It remains a local lead,
not a completed callee note. The principal stopped the process afterward,
exit 255. No guest state was changed by the register hooks.

The comparison and input-mask arithmetic are now recorded. Selection use,
the alternate Up block and the unnamed helper remain open. No complete
game function was implemented or promoted by this research.

The Up branch at `0x800650d0` is recorded in
