Traced fact from the USA PS-X EXE. Re-check on a disc you own with `python3 tools/entry_disasm.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x8005c620`. The executable and the listing stay in `build/`.

The start address is `0x8005c620`. [call-8005c5e8.md](call-8005c5e8.md) branches here. `s1` is `0x30`. `s5` is `0x30`. `s3` and `s4` are `0x800e54e0`. `a0` is `0x800e5510`. `s6` is 1. `fp` is 0.

The byte at `0x800e5510` is `0x30`, so `beq` at `0x8005c628` is not taken. The byte at `0x800e5531` is 1, so `bne` at `0x8005c63c` is taken. `s1` becomes `0x60` and `s5` becomes `0x60`. That sum is below `0x800`. Control returns to `0x8005c620`. The next note is that arrival.
