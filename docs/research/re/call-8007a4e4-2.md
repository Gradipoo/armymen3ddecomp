# Second call at 0x8007a4e4

Traced fact from the USA PS-X EXE. Re-check on a disc you own with `python3 tools/entry_disasm.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x8007a4e4`. The executable and the listing stay in `build/`.

The start address is `0x8007a4e4`. The `jal` in [call-8007a570-2.md](call-8007a570-2.md) jumps here. `a0` is `0x8005bf80`. `ra` is `0x8007a5a4`. The frame at `0x800251f8` stays open. Its saved `ra` stays `0x8007faec`.

The loads at `0x8007a4ec` and `0x8007a4f0` are the same words as [call-8007a4e4.md](call-8007a4e4.md). `a1` starts at `0x80010000`. `a3` is `0x8001007c`. `a2` starts at 0. The loop compares the word at each pointer with `a0`.

The match is the pointer `0x800b3b68` from the slot at `0x80010004`. The word at `0x800b3b68` is `0x8005bf80`. After that copy, `a2` is not 0, so `beq` at `0x8007a530` is not taken. `jr` at `0x8007a538` returns through `ra`. The delay slot copies `a2` into `v0`. `v0` leaves as `0x800b3b68`. `ra` leaves as `0x8007a5a4`.

Control goes to `0x8007a5a4`. The caller return at `0x8007faec` is not traced.
