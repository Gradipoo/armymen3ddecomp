# Block at 0x80070fb0

Traced fact from the USA PS-X EXE. Re-check on a disc you own with `python3 tools/entry_disasm.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x80070fb0`. The executable and the listing stay in `build/`.

The start address is `0x80070fb0`. The `jal` in [call-8006447c.md](call-8006447c.md) jumps here. `ra` is `0x8006448c`. `a0` is `0x80010018`, `a1` is `0x8001005c`, `a2` is `0x800b5d78`, and `a3` is `0x8001007c`. The frame at `0x800251f8` stays open. Its saved `ra` stays `0x8007faec`.

The 92 bytes from `0x80070fb0` through `0x8007100b` match the 92 bytes at `0x8006f12c`. [call-8006f12c.md](call-8006f12c.md) already records those stores. `t0` becomes `0x1f800380` and `t1` becomes `0x200`. The same words are written: `0x200` at `0x1f800388`, `0x1f80039c`, and `0x1f8003b0`, and 0 at the other words from `0x1f800380` through `0x1f8003c8`. The delay slot of the `jr` writes the last 0.

`jr` at `0x80071004` returns through `ra`. `v0` is not written. `a0`, `a1`, `a2`, and `a3` stay as they arrived. `ra` leaves as `0x8006448c`.

Control goes to `0x8006448c`. The caller return at `0x8007faec` is not traced.
