# Call at 0x80073c2c

No traced entry reaches this address. There is no branch and no call.

`a1` becomes `a1 + 3`, then bits below 4 are cleared, then 4 is added. `mult` multiplies that by `a0`. `mflo` keeps the low 32 bits. `jr ra` returns that product plus `0x14`. The add wraps in 32 bits.

For `a0` 0 and `a1` 0, the aligned `a1` is 4 and `v0` is `0x14`. For `a0` 1 and `a1` 1, the aligned `a1` is 8 and `v0` is `0x1c`.

The 32 bytes from `0x80073c2c` through `0x80073c48` occur once.
