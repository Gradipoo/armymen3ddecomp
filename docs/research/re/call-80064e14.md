# Fallthrough at 0x80064e14

[call-80064dec.md](call-80064dec.md) and [call-80064dfc.md](call-80064dfc.md) record
the shared `beq` at `0x80064e0c` when the word at `sp+0x1c` is nonzero. The
fallthrough lands here. The delay slot at `0x80064e10` already cleared `s7` in
those blocks. This note does not resume the parked static boot.

`python3 tools/boot_slice.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x80064e14 --max 64`
reports 21 instructions and stops at the unknown `beq` at `0x80064e60`. The
report stays in `build/`.

## Our code

`components/exe/call_80064e14.cpp` records `s0` and `s4`, optionally stores
`s5 * 4` through `stack_word_28`, then calls `call_80064e40` for the shared
loop head including the portable follow for the `beq` at `0x80064e60`.
Glue passes `MenuInputScanState::index` as `s5`.

`menu_input_scan_step` calls this block when
[call-80064dfc](call-80064dfc.md) or [call-80064dec](call-80064dec.md) records
`entered_64e14`. Glue supplies `stack_word_18`, cleared `s7`, optional
`stack_word_28`, and the same injectable `PadGetState` hook as the main loop.
The re-entry `head` records the same `branch_to_64f14` or `entered_64e68`
side as the primary loop head without guessing live words.

`python3 tools/boot_slice.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x80064e60 --max 32`
reports the branch and stops. Taken is `0x80064f14`. Fallthrough is
`0x80064e68`. Static boot does not state the side.

## Gap

Live words at `sp+0x18` and `sp+0x28` on this path are not stated. The
`PadGetState` body is not entered. Glue does not enter `0x80064f14` or
[call-80064e68](call-80064e68.md) after this re-entry block.
