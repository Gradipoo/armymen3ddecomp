# Block at 0x80086c2c

Traced fact from the USA PS-X EXE. Re-check on a disc you own with `python3 tools/entry_disasm.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x80086c2c`. The executable and the listing stay in `build/`.

The start address is `0x80086c2c`. `python3 tools/sdk_match.py` names it `PutDrawEnv`. The `jal` in [call-800761dc.md](call-800761dc.md) calls here and sets `ra` to `0x80076220`. Its delay slot sets `a0` to `0x800e15f0`. On arrival `a0` is `0x800e15f0`. `s0` is `0x800e15f0`. `s1` is `0x800e15b0`. `s2` is 0. [call-8008aafc.md](call-8008aafc.md) has stored the draw environment at `0x800e15f0`. The byte at `0x800b75ca` is 0, so `sltiu` against 2 leaves `v0` at 1 and `bne` is taken. The tool stopped at that `bne` because it did not have the byte. This note follows the taken side and stops at the first unnamed `jal`. The hard cap is 64 instructions. This block ended before the cap.

`addiu` at `0x80086c2c` opens a frame of `0x20` and saves `s0`, `s1`, `s2`, and `ra`. `s2` becomes `0x800b75ca`. `lbu` loads the byte at that address. It is 0. `bne` at `0x80086c54` is taken. The delay slot copies `a0` into `s1`, so `s1` is `0x800e15f0`.

`addiu` at `0x80086c78` sets `s0` to `0x800e160c`. `jal` at `0x80086c80` targets `0x8008760c` and sets `ra` to `0x80086c88`. The delay slot copies `s1` into `a1`, so `a1` is `0x800e15f0`. `a0` is `0x800e160c`. `python3 tools/sdk_match.py` does not name `0x8008760c`. This note does not enter that body.

After the delay slot, control goes to `0x8008760c`. `a0` is `0x800e160c`. `a1` is `0x800e15f0`. `s0` is `0x800e160c`. `s1` is `0x800e15f0`. `s2` is `0x800b75ca`. `ra` is `0x80086c88`. The words at `0x80086c88` are not traced. The display x and y halfwords are still not quoted. This note does not close the gap in `components/exe/load.cpp`.
