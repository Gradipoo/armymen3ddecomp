# Block at 0x800883ec

Traced fact from the USA PS-X EXE. Re-check on a disc you own with `python3 tools/entry_disasm.py build/disc/SLUS_004.91 --start 0x800883ec`. The executable and the listing stay in `build/`.

The start address is `0x800883ec`. The `beq` in [call-800883d4.md](call-800883d4.md) is this instruction. On a DuckStation fast boot of the USA cue, the first stop at this PC is on that path. `ra` is `0x800883d4`. `s0` is `0x800e160c`. `s1` is `0x40`. `s2` is 0. `s3` is `0x800882c4`. `a0` is `0x800b75c8`. `v0` is 1. `v1` is 1. The byte at `0x800b75c9` is 1. The word at `0x800b76f8` is `0xd`. The word at `0x800b75d0` is 0 at this stop, before the delay slot. The words at `0x800b76f0` and `0x800b76f4` are 0. The word at `0x800b76dc` is `0x1f8010a8`. The word at `0x800b75d4` is 0. A later stop at this same `beq` still had byte 1, and the word at `0x800b75d0` was 1. The hard cap is 64 instructions. This block ended before the cap.

`beq` at `0x800883ec` branches to `0x80088440` when `v1` is 0. `v1` is 1, so this boot falls through. The delay slot stores 1 at `0x800b75d0`. `lw` loads the word at `0x800b76f0` into `v1` and the word at `0x800b76f4` into `v0`. Both are 0. `bne` at `0x80088408` branches to `0x80088484` when they differ. They do not, so this boot falls through. The delay slot is `nop`. `lw` loads `0x1f8010a8` from `0x800b76dc` into `v1`, then loads the word at that address into `v0`. This note does not quote that word. `and` keeps the `0x1000000` bit. At a stop on `bne` at `0x80088428`, `v0` is 0 and `v1` is `0x1000000`. That `bne` branches to `0x80088484` when `v0` is not 0. This boot falls through. The delay slot is `nop`. `lw` loads the word at `0xc(a0)`, address `0x800b75d4`, into `v0`. That word is 0. `bne` at `0x80088438` branches to `0x80088484` when `v0` is not 0. This boot falls through. The delay slot is `nop`.

Control then reaches `0x80088440`. `lw` loads the word at `0x800b76d0` into `v1`. This note does not quote that word. `lui` sets `a0` to `0x4000000`. `lw` loads the word at `0(v1)`. `and` keeps the `0x4000000` bit. `beq` at `0x80088458` branches to `0x8008844c` when that result is 0. This note does not quote that bit, so the side is unknown.

After the delay slot of the last cited branch, `s0`, `s1`, `s2`, and `s3` stay as they were at `0x800883ec`. The word at `0x800b75d0` is 1. The words at `0x8008844c` and `0x8008845c` are not traced. The display x and y halfwords are still not quoted. This note does not close the gap in `components/exe/load.cpp`.
